# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analysis project exploring customer purchasing patterns, demographics, product preferences, and subscription behaviors across 3,900 transaction records.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project processes and analyzes retail transactional data to extract actionable business insights. Using Python for data cleaning and feature engineering, MySQL for relational queries, and Power BI for interactive dashboard visualization, the analysis helps guide revenue growth, customer retention, and strategic marketing decisions.

---

## 📊 Dataset Summary
* **Records:** 3,900 purchases
* **Features:** 18 attributes
* **Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status
* **Purchase Details:** Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount (USD), Season, Size, Color
* **Behavioral Attributes:** Discount Applied, Promo Code Used, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Shipping Type
* **Data Quality Note:** Imputed 37 missing values in `Review Rating` using category-level median ratings.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Python (`pandas`, `numpy`)
* **Database & Querying:** MySQL
* **Data Visualization:** Power BI

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow & Methodology

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation (Python)
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Inspected data structure (`.info()`, `.describe()`) and identified missing values.
* **Missing Value Imputation:** Filled 37 missing `Review Rating` values using the median rating per product category.
* **Standardization:** Converted column names to `snake_case` for SQL compatibility and consistency.
* **Feature Engineering:**
  * Binned customer ages into categorical `age_group` values.
  * Derived `purchase_frequency_days` from frequency metrics.
* **Redundancy Removal:** Verified collinearity/redundancy between `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used`; dropped `promo_code_used`.
* **Database Loading:** Exported the clean dataset to PostgreSQL via SQLAlchemy.

---

### 2. Business Analysis (MySQL)
Executed structured queries to address core business questions:
1. **Gender Revenue Split:** Aggregated total revenue contribution by gender.
2. **High-Value Discount Users:** Identified customers using discounts whose total spend exceeded the overall average purchase amount.
3. **Top-Rated Products:** Ranked products by average customer review rating.
4. **Shipping Performance:** Compared average purchase values between Standard, Express, and alternative shipping options.
5. **Subscription Impact:** Evaluated spend differences and total revenue generated by subscribers vs. non-subscribers.
6. **Discount Dependency:** Identified top 5 products with the highest proportion of discounted sales.
7. **Customer Segmentation:** Classified users into *New*, *Returning*, and *Loyal* tiers based on historical purchase counts.
8. **Category Leaders:** Determined the top 3 most purchased items per product category using window functions.
9. **Repeat Purchase Rates:** Analyzed subscription conversion rates for high-frequency buyers (>5 purchases).
10. **Demographic Revenue:** Evaluated revenue distribution across age groups.

---

### 3. Interactive Visualization (Power BI)
Built an interactive dashboard featuring key operational KPIs and interactive slicers:
* **Key Metrics:** Total Customers (3.9K), Average Spend ($59.76), Average Review Rating (3.75).
* **Subscription Breakdown:** Visualized revenue distribution across subscriber tiers.
* **Sales & Revenue by Category:** Bar charts depicting volume and total revenue per product category.
* **Demographic Breakdown:** Revenue segmented by age brackets and gender.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Filterable by Subscription Status, Gender, Category, and Shipping Type.

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Business Recommendations
* **Subscription Drive:** Convert one-time and returning customers by offering targeted incentives, as non-subscribers currently comprise the majority of the customer base.
* **Loyalty & Retention Programs:** Structure rewards programs around repeat buyers (>5 purchases) to transition them into high-value loyal segments.
* **Discount Optimization:** Audit high-discount products to protect profit margins while maintaining sales velocity.
* **Inventory & Marketing Focus:** Prioritize marketing spend on top-rated items (e.g., Gloves, Sandals, Boots) and high-revenue age groups.
